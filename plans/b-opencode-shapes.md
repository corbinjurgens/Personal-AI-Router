## Goal

Qualify the OpenCode Chat Completions shapes the MSI daily strong-tier target needs: tools including history-only empty definitions, and automatic reasoning replay under the proposed owner-approved qualification boundary. A strict request using an undeclared shape fails with 400 `strict-qualification` before admission, wake, unload, capacity markers or inference. Profiles advertising tools/structured without matching qualification do not install. Synthetic tests establish the contract without a real client/model run. NE Task 4 supplies trustworthy model/content/config identity; B consumes that identity and owns request shape validation/accounting.
